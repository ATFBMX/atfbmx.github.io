Who We Are

We’re a community of Toronto flatland riders. We ride, mentor, run jams, and keep the scene moving.

What We Do

How we grow the scene

🛹

Open practice & mentoring

Regular meetups and open-floor sessions for all levels. Riders mentor newcomers, trade ropework, and push each other forward.

🎉

Jams, demos & events

We organize and support local jams, demos, and collaborations with Toronto’s BMX community and local orgs.

🚲

Beginner support

Setup advice, safety basics, and clear progression starting points — get rolling on flatland with real guidance.
